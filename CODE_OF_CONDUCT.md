# Code of Conduct

This project follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

In short: be respectful, assume good faith, give constructive feedback, and keep
discussion about the work. Harassment, insults and personal attacks aren't tolerated.

## Reporting

To report unacceptable behavior, contact the maintainer
[@Dead-Done](https://github.com/Dead-Done) privately through GitHub. Reports are
handled confidentially. The maintainer may remove comments, close issues or block
users who violate this code.
